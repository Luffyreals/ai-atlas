## Formulas

Inline formula: the prediction $\hat{y} = \mathbf{w}^\top \mathbf{x} + b$, and a comparison $a < b$.

Display formula:

$$
\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}, b) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} \left( \hat{y}^{(i)} - y^{(i)} \right)^2
$$
